FAQ

Common questions about
Tradovate copy trading

Everything you need to know before connecting your Tradovate accounts to SimpleTrader.

Does SimpleTrader support all Tradovate account types?
Yes. SimpleTrader works with all Tradovate account types: personal live accounts, demo accounts, and funded accounts from any prop firm that uses Tradovate — including Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, Elite Trader Funding, Take Profit Trader, and others.
How does the Tradovate copy trading work technically?
SimpleTrader connects to Tradovate's official WebSocket API. It monitors the master account in real time and, the moment an order is placed or filled, sends the identical order to all configured slave accounts simultaneously. No polling, no delay — just direct API calls at exchange speed.
Can I use SimpleTrader to copy trades to multiple prop firm accounts?
Yes. This is the primary use case. Trade on one personal Tradovate account and replicate automatically to Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, Elite Trader Funding, Take Profit Trader, and any other Tradovate-based prop firm — all simultaneously in a single subscription.
Is there a limit to how many Tradovate accounts I can copy to?
No. SimpleTrader places no limit on the number of slave accounts. You can copy to 2 accounts or 50 accounts on the same subscription. Each account can have its own independent scaling settings.
Does SimpleTrader work while I have Tradovate open in my browser?
Yes. SimpleTrader runs independently on cloud infrastructure and does not interfere with your Tradovate session. You can trade normally on Tradovate Web or Desktop while SimpleTrader replicates silently in the background.
What futures instruments does the Tradovate copier support?
SimpleTrader supports all futures instruments available on Tradovate: equity index futures (ES, NQ, YM, RTY and micros), energy futures (CL, NG), metals (GC, SI), treasury futures (ZB, ZN), and forex futures (6E, 6B, 6J). If Tradovate supports the instrument, SimpleTrader can replicate it.
