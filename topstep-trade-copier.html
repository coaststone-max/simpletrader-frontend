FAQ

Common questions about
the Topstep Trade Copier

Everything you need to know before connecting your Topstep accounts.

Does SimpleTrader work with all Topstep account types?
Yes. SimpleTrader works with all Topstep funded accounts traded on Tradovate — Express, Standard, and any evaluation or live funded account. Both the evaluation phase and funded phase are supported without any change in configuration.
Do I need to install any EA or plugin?
No. SimpleTrader connects directly to the Tradovate API. There is no EA, no NinjaTrader plugin, no TradingView alert workaround — just a clean browser-based connection that works automatically without any platform installation.
How fast does SimpleTrader replicate trades to Topstep accounts?
Typical replication latency is under 120ms via persistent WebSocket connections. Orders are placed simultaneously to all slave accounts the moment the master order is confirmed — not after a polling delay or webhook roundtrip.
Can I scale position size differently across my Topstep accounts?
Yes. Each slave account can use a fixed multiplier or a custom lot size. For example, you can trade 1 contract on the master, 1 contract on a $50k Topstep account, and 2 contracts on a $150k account — all configured independently per account.
Does SimpleTrader have access to my Topstep capital?
No. SimpleTrader uses read+trade API permissions only — never withdrawal access. Your capital stays fully in your Topstep account. SimpleTrader can place and close orders, but cannot move, withdraw, or transfer any funds.
What happens if my internet drops while replication is active?
SimpleTrader runs on cloud infrastructure, not your local machine. Even if your internet drops, the replication engine continues running in the cloud and processes trades from your master account without interruption. When reconnected, the dashboard syncs immediately.
