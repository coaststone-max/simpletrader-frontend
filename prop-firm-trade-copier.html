FAQ

Common questions about
prop firm trade copying

Everything you need to know before scaling your prop firm operation with SimpleTrader.

Which prop firms does SimpleTrader support?
SimpleTrader supports all prop firms that use Tradovate as their execution platform. This includes Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, Elite Trader Funding, Take Profit Trader, Bulenox, TradeDay, and any other firm running on Tradovate. If your prop firm account is traded on Tradovate, SimpleTrader can replicate to it.
Can I copy to multiple different prop firms simultaneously?
Yes. This is exactly what SimpleTrader is designed for. Trade once on your master Tradovate account and replicate simultaneously to Apex, Topstep, Elite Trader Funding, Take Profit Trader — all at the same time, all in under 120ms average latency.
Does SimpleTrader work during prop firm evaluations?
Yes. SimpleTrader works during evaluation phases and live funded phases for all supported prop firms. You can copy to evaluation accounts to pass the combine, then continue copying to the funded account after passing — no reconfiguration needed.
Will prop firms know I'm using a trade copier?
SimpleTrader places orders through the official Tradovate API on each account independently. Orders appear as normal API-placed orders on each account — the same as placing them manually. Always review your specific prop firm's terms of service regarding automated trading tools.
Can I set different lot sizes for each prop firm account?
Yes. Each slave account has its own lot multiplier. Trade 2 contracts on master, copy 1 contract to your $50k Apex account, 2 contracts to your $100k Topstep account, and 3 contracts to your $150k Elite account — all configured independently per account.
How is SimpleTrader different from TradingView webhooks?
TradingView webhooks typically add 2–8 seconds of latency and require a separate bridge service to connect to Tradovate. SimpleTrader connects directly to the Tradovate WebSocket API, monitoring your master account live and replicating orders in under 120ms — with no intermediate webhook service, no EA, and no additional setup.
