Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

add
Start with full Pro access for 7 days — no credit card required. At the end of the trial you can choose any plan or continue with Starter for free.

Do I need coding knowledge?

add
Zero. SimpleTrader connects directly to Tradovate's API. You just enter your credentials, link your accounts, and the replicator does the rest.

Which prop firms are supported?

add
Any prop firm that uses Tradovate as their trading platform: Apex Trader Funding, Topstep, Elite Trader Funding, TakeProfitTrader, Bulenox, and more. If it runs on Tradovate, SimpleTrader works.

Can I change plans later?

add
Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect immediately and billing is prorated.

How is such low latency achieved?

add
SimpleTrader's replication engine polls your master account every 2.5 seconds via Tradovate's WebSocket API and dispatches copy orders in parallel to all slave accounts. Elite plans run on dedicated infrastructure co-located with Tradovate servers.
